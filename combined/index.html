Intrinsic Value in Everyday Object

This project investigates price volatility in relation to currency through the lens of everyday objects — using the banana as a central medium. It examines how fluctuations in economic systems quietly shape our emotional relationships with commodities we encounter daily.

01

Banana Poem Generator

Hold a banana to your camera. A poem is generated in real time from the words of the project essays, responding to the proximity of the banana.

02

Price Volatility Simulator

How many minutes of work does it take to afford the same item? Compare Tokyo and New York City side by side using wage-to-price ratios to make purchasing power feel immediate and personal.
